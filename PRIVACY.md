# Tabbernate Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Tabbernate is a Chrome extension that shows your open tabs in the browser's side panel so you can search, sort, switch to, close and put tabs to sleep.

## What data Tabbernate accesses

To display and manage your tabs, Tabbernate reads information about your open tabs through Chrome's `tabs` API:

- tab titles and URLs
- tab favicons
- whether a tab is active or asleep, and when it was last used

## How that data is used

This information is used **only inside your browser**, to show the list of tabs in the side panel and to carry out the actions you choose (switching to, closing, or sleeping a tab).

## What Tabbernate does not do

- It does **not** collect, store or keep a history of your tabs or browsing activity.
- It does **not** send any data to the developer or to any server. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services.
- It does **not** sell or share any data with anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
